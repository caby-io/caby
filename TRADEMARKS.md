# Caby Trademark Policy

The Caby **source code** is licensed under the [GNU AGPL v3](LICENSE). However, the **"Caby" name
and the Caby logo** are trademarks of the Caby project. The AGPL grants you broad rights to the
code; it grants **no rights to the name or logo**.

The goal is to ensure free, open-source access to Caby's software and source code, while protecting
the Caby name and logo from being used to mislead our users.

## You may, without asking

- **Run and self-host** Caby as you please. This includes forked or modified versions.
- **Redistribute unmodified builds/releases** (binaries, container images, etc.) with the "Caby"
  name and logo.
- **Refer to Caby by name** to describe your work truthfully, in a blog post, a comparison, or
  criticism (e.g. "compatible with Caby", "runs on Caby", "a Caby client", "a better alternative to
  Caby").
- **Provide services around Caby** such as hosting, templates, support services, etc., so long as
  you **do not** present yourself or your organization as an official or endorsed Caby project.

## You may not, without permission

- **Distribute a modified version under the Caby name or logo.** A fork or modified build must use
  its own name and drop the Caby logo; you may still state factually that it is "based on Caby" or
  "a fork of Caby", but the product's name must be yours, not ours.
- **Name a product, service, company, or domain "Caby"**, e.g. "Caby Cloud", a "Caby" appliance,
  `caby-*.com`, or something confusingly similar.
- **Sell Caby-branded hardware, appliances, or a hosted service.** The Caby name on a commercial
  product implies it's ours; that requires a commercial or trademark agreement with us.
- **Use the Caby logo** in any modified or altered form, or in any way that implies endorsement,
  affiliation, or official status.
